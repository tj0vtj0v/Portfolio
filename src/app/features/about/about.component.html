Development, research and lots of ideas.

Hi, I'm Tjorven. I'm studying Artificial Intelligence at the Deggendorf Institute of Technology. In my free time, I like building things, trying out ideas, and figuring out how they work.

I'm especially interested in computer vision, robotics, and software that has to work in the real world. At Fast Forest, I have been working as part of a large team developing a race car. I build submodules for its autonomous system. This creates an interesting combination: solving technical problems while working with people who bring different skills and perspectives. I enjoy that the team cares about progress and performance, but also about making sure that everyone feels like being home.

My side projects have taught me something different: progress is not always linear. I've been building and flying FPV drones for years, although there have been some longer breaks when I didn't have enough time or motivation. I'm also turning my idea of designing and building my own VTOL aircraft into a long-term project. It is bigger than something I can finish all at once, but I enjoy working on it step by step and seeing how it develops over time.
