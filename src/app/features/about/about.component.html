About me

Development, research,
and a little curiosity.

I'm Tjorven, an artificial intelligence student at the Deggendorf Institute of Technology. I like understanding how things work, trying ideas out, and learning from what happens along the way.

Much of my work sits where software meets the physical world: helping a race car recognise cones, building maps from sensor observations, or processing aerial imagery. I also build web tools that help me organise and evaluate everyday data.

I enjoy working through these problems with other people. There is always something new to learn — whether that is an algorithm, a different way of thinking, or a lesson from testing.
