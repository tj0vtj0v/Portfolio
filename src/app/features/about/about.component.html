A little about me...

Hi, I’m Tjorven, I study artificial intelligence at the Deggendorf Institute of Technology and spend much of my free time building software or working on things that fly. Through my work in the driverless department at Fast Forest, I discovered my interest in computer vision and robotics.

What I enjoy there goes beyond developing the car. Working with people from different fields means there’s always someone with another perspective to work through a problem with. We put a lot into getting the car ready and making it faster, but the people are just as much a reason to stay. With all the long days and nights in the workshop and at events, the team has become like a second home.

Outside Formula Student, I’ve been building and flying FPV drones for years. Some projects get a lot of my time, others sit for a while until I feel like picking them up again. My own VTOL is one of those longer-term ideas. Seeing how my other projects have come along over the years gives me confidence that the VTOL will eventually reach a point where I have something to show. There’s still plenty I need to learn for it, but I’m happy to work through that bit by bit.

All of those projects keep me at a desk most of the time, apart from testing. When I need a change of pace, I head outdoors for a run, spend a day hiking, or go skiing in the winter.
